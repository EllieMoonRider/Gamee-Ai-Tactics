# Puzzles & Conquest — Intel Dossier
Verified P&C-specific intel. Sources: General Ellie (source-of-truth), Paksen's Puzzles & Conquest channel (2023), Froth1k channel (2022). NO cross-reference with P&Survival, P&Chaos, Merge Dragons, or other titles.

## Game Overview
Mythology-themed match-3 strategy. City-builder + kingdom warfare + match-3 combat hybrid. Alliances for protection. Outside the city: resources, monsters, hell fortresses (daily quests). **Altar of Dragonia** = central map objective — alliances fight for control; holder gains kingdom-leader bonuses. **Hero Hall** to recruit heroes. Heroes: level up + combine to strengthen. Match-3 mini-game: each hero has a color = matching gem type; 3+ in a row/column attacks enemies.

Tagline: "Crush, Construct, Conquer".

## Alliance Help / Speedup
- Teammates speed up your building/training/researching/healing at NO cost to you.
- **~1 minute reduction per help** (varies with help efficiency).
- Reward for helping others: **800 Alliance Coins per help** · daily cap 40,000.
- Daily Quest: help 10× = 5 pts.
- **Speedup Help Cap** = max teammates that can help ONE task, governed ONLY by Embassy level:
  - No Embassy: 2 helpers
  - Embassy L1: 4 helpers · each upgrade increases both count + efficiency
- **CRITICAL:** Cap is locked at Alliance Hall level when the task STARTED, NOT current level.

## Territory Overview + Held vs Bag Resources
Territory Overview reports each resource's: Held · Daily Cap · Hourly Output · In Bag.

**Held Resources** = "ready cash" — pays first, **plunderable** above Storage limit.
- Ways to reduce held: spend · send to Alliance Storage · Mystery Shop exchange (moves to Bag).
- Ways held increases: gather (Perimeter/World Map) · withdraw from Alliance Storage · Fast Producer talent · Goddess Statue Wish · Alliance gift (huge tax).

**Total in Bag** = safe/unraidable. Must be withdrawn to spend.
- Bag increases via: event rewards · Mystery Shop · Rare Earth Shop · Alliance Gifts.

**Daily Cap** = only 10 hours' worth of hourly output (out of 24).
**Fast Producer talent:** 5× hourly output, does NOT count against Daily Cap. Activatable 2× daily = effectively doubles the cap.

## Ace Lord — 6-Phase Weekly Framework

**Golden Rule:** HOARD. Never touch upgrades or use speedups until the designated day.

| Phase | Focus | Prep |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Gathering | Resources on map | Empty army into high-level tiles night before; return at reset |

## Major Events

### Dragonia War (Alliance / PvP)
- Center map objective: **Altar of Dragonia** — the most important landmark in the kingdom.
- Alliances fight to occupy + hold. Holder gets **Kingdom Leader** title + alliance-wide buffs.
- Contested periodically (NOT daily).
- **Combat Event** — War Room priority: troop health, hero PvP synergy, reinforcement capacity. NOT resource-gathering.
- App must flag Active Combat state during timer; trigger high-alert notification for alliance.
- Reinforcement logistics: distance + travel time from members to central map matters.
- **⚠️ Cross-title trap:** "Dragonia" also used in Merge Dragons for different mechanics. Red flags for wrong-game context: merge-style mini-game · "Dragon Cups" → NOT P&C.

### Demon Incursion (PvE)
- Waves of demons attack alliance castles.
- **Alliance-optimal play:** stronger players send massive reinforcements to weakest/lowest-level members' castles → keeping weak castles from burning out early maximizes ALLIANCE + personal scores.
- **Personal Rank 1 tactic (Froth1k):**
  - Difficulty: Level 7 or Level 11. Level 8 = waste.
  - Set farms ~250-260 km from castle · pre-reinforce farms with each other (locks out other players).
  - Wait for **Wave 17 (elite)** to reach farms.
  - Use **Recall Talent** to pull troops back fast; send to castles/farms → defend again (faster than most demon marches).
  - **Wave 14 = elite** · **Wave 17 = elite** — highest point tiers.
  - After Wave 17: most points scored, pull back.
  - DO NOT waste diamonds on Direct Relocators or speedups. Rank 1 = 5,000 Diamonds.
- Event cadence: ~2 weeks.

### Battle of Saurnesia (Cross-Server / Alliance)
- Cross-server matching event; alliances on isolated map compete for key resource buildings.
- Points from: **Holding Wonders** (Ancient Tower, Divine Fountain) = static · **Dragon Cub Escort mechanic** = largest point spikes (guard/capture points along path).
- Coordinate alliance entry windows.
- **Battlefield Infirmary mindset:** troops safely returned afterward → do NOT fear aggressive high-tier rallies to push enemies out of 3-min wonder captures.

### Monster Hunt
- Coordinate hunting paths using the **Hunt Bonus chain multiplier**.
- Never solo isolated high-level monsters.
- Match stamina expenditures into continuous alliance rally loops on Hell Fortresses → multiplies point returns per stamina.

### Timerift
- Wave-based enemy encounter. Enemy damage + your damage both scale per round.
- Target: Wave 20 minimum · Wave 50 ideal.
- Reference benchmarks (Froth1k): Same-week Rank 1 ~9M · Prior-week Rank 1 ~50M · Rank 10 ~18M · personal best 60M+200K over 3 days.

### Boneyard
- Points-based alliance-driven competitive event.
- **Catapult mechanic:** if scouted by a catapult, target waits 45 seconds before seeing incoming march contents — used to burn rival time.

### Feast Event
- **Player cap: 3,000 seats** — arrive on time.
- Variants: Common Feast · Heart Feast (and others).
- Everyone piles into middle → chests drop.
- Can leave and come back during event.
- Timer visible top-right corner.
- Rewards: **Startup Blessings** + **Glories** *(currency types)*.

## Arena — Hero Matchup Rules
- Color matchup = rock/paper/scissors (Blue vs Red = strong matchup, etc.).
- **Poseidon: DEFENSE ONLY** — never use on attack.
- **Sun Wukong**: HP support hero.
- **Combo — Animus + Nefertari:** Animus can still load MP after using his skill → unbeatable in same round.
- **Combo — Nezha + Animus:** running pair.
- Prep threshold: hero at **+5 with 1 red equipment** minimum before serious arena play.
- **Hero Championship** = top Arena rankers qualify. PRIMARY source of Troop Gems.

## Formation Research (Institute Level 28 unlock)
- Front-line troop type determines damage taken.
- Rally leader can select formation type (which troop is in front).
- Real numbers: **12K Infantry front + Cavalry mix = 81% win rate** vs Cavalry-only = 79%.
- Only Infantry provides real front-line defense.
- Cavalry: defense between Infantry and Range.
- Range: highest damage, squishy.

## Hell Fortress Rally Composition
- **Cavalry:** ~60% victory rate needed to advance to next level target.
- **Range / Infantry:** ~65-70% victory needed.
- Cavalry lives longer + deals more damage than Range.
- **Best hybrid rally:** Infantry + Range (Inf tanks, Range deals damage).

## Alliance Conquest — Battle Phase (verified)
Battle phase lasts **24 hours**. Each participating player gets **2 attack windows** in that period.
- **Each successful attack = 100 Alliance Points.**
- Only the **30 players selected** by alliance leadership can participate in the battle phase.
- Non-participating members see a **Spectate** button to watch battles in real time — they cannot attack or reinforce during the battle phase.

**Line-up Shield System:**
- **Green shield** on an enemy line-up = you can attack it.
- **Red shield** on an enemy line-up = already defeated, locked out, cannot be attacked again.
- Player picks WHICH enemy line-up to fight.
- **Win** the fight → their shield turns **red** (line-up removed from attackable pool) + your alliance banks the 100 points.
- **Lose** the fight → you burn 1 attack attempt · alliance scores 0 · shield stays **green** · that line-up remains attackable by others.

**Line-up Rule (defender side):**
- Each defender fields **2 line-ups**. Each can only be used **once**.
- If your first line-up already fought, you can't reuse it — the second line-up must defend.
- When **both** your line-ups are defeated, you're **locked out** of further attacks against you (no more incoming).

**Battle Record:**
- Post-phase log shows who fought whom, who won, who lost — accountability tool for both alliances.
- Use it to identify weak links inside your own alliance AND top threats inside the enemy alliance for next cycle.

- Strategy: burn both attacks on high-value targets to maximize the 200-point per-player ceiling. Coordinate rally targets via alliance chat before battle-phase open to avoid double-hitting the same target. Study battle records after each cycle to refine matchmaking — don't send weak line-ups against known enemy tanks.
