## Game Overview
Mythology-themed match-3 strategy. City-builder + kingdom warfare + match-3 combat hybrid. Alliances for protection. Outside the city: resources, monsters, hell fortresses (daily quests). **Altar of Dragonia** = central map objective — alliances fight for control; holder gains kingdom-leader bonuses. **Hero Hall** to recruit heroes. Heroes: level up + combine to strengthen. Match-3 mini-game: each hero has a color = matching gem type; 3+ in a row/column attacks enemies.

Tagline: "Crush, Construct, Conquer".

## Troop Type Doctrine (VERIFIED)

**4 Troop Types**: Infantry · Cavalry · Ranged (Archer) · Siege.

**Rock-Paper-Scissors Matrix**:
- **Infantry** > **Ranged/Archer** > **Cavalry** > **Infantry** (cycle).
- **Infantry / Archer / Cavalry** all > **Siege**.

**INFANTRY**:
- High defense + high health, **lowest attack** of the 3 main types.
- Best for **overall defense** — structures, castle, front-line tanking.
- Strong against **Archers** · Weak against **Cavalry**.

**CAVALRY**:
- Balanced attack/defense/health + **higher march speed** (mobility advantage).
- Good for **defense AND attack** — extra speed to hit objectives faster than Infantry/Archers.
- Strong against **Infantry** · Weak against **Archers**.

**ARCHERS (Ranged)**:
- **High attack**, low defense + health.
- Best for **capturing objectives** and **castle attacks**.
- Strong against **Cavalry** · Weak against **Infantry**.

**SIEGE**:
- **Large carrying capacity** — gather resource tiles with fewer troops OR plunder castles once defenders are wiped.
- Do NOT rally with (see earlier: Siege gets NO bonus in Archer rallies).

## Formation Preview — Hall of War (VERIFIED)
Unlock: **Castle 20**. The **Formation Preview** feature appears in the **Hall of War**.

**What it does**: Select your formation AND the opponent's formation → preview the exact troop positions during the coming battle. Lets you pre-visualize matchups before committing.

**Where the formation selector appears**:
- Player's troop **marching screen** (outgoing march)
- **Wall defense screen** (defenders)
- **Pre-formation group screen** (before rally launches)

**Default formation**: **Infantry** (used automatically until you change it).

**Battle Report identification**: The **icon of the formation used** appears on the battle report — audit which formation was used post-fight.

### Unlocking Other Formations
Ranger and Cavalry formations require **Institute Lv.28** and are researched in **Technology → Marching Formation**:
- **Rangers Formation** — unlocks Ranger positioning bonuses.
- **Cavalry Formation** — unlocks Cavalry positioning bonuses.

**Doctrine**: Once Institute 28 lands, research both formation types even if you're specialized in one. Post-Institute-28 you can:
- Deploy Ranger formation with a Ranger-heavy march for max Ranger positioning.
- Deploy Cavalry formation for speed + positioning stacked.
- Deploy Infantry formation (default) for tank front-line configurations.

**Verify formation used** via battle report icon after every major fight — if your formation didn't switch as intended, that's a critical bug in your setup to fix before the next engagement.

## Specialization Doctrine — Focus One Troop Type (VERIFIED, Raider Community)

**Core principle**: Specializing (focusing all buffs, gear, and hero investment on **ONE troop type**) is one of the biggest battle-strength multipliers in the game.

### Math Behind Specialization
- Mixed-army approach: 3 troop types, each at ~**400% ATK** = balanced but capped.
- Specialized approach: 1 troop type at **700% ATK**, others sit at ~300% = **massively higher per-march damage**.
- No brainer: you send **1 full march of your chosen troop type + heroes matching that type** = 700% wins.

### Which Troop to Specialize In?

**Options**: Cavalry · Ranged · Infantry.
**NOT** Siege — no gear or heroes exist to specialize Siege stats.

**Hidden-stat truth (verified via game coding, replicates in 2 other same-dev mobile war games)**:
- **Traps DO NOT work** (game-taught weakness triangles are essentially cosmetic).
- **Infantry** — slightly better DEF, lower ATK.
- **Ranged** — better ATK, lower DEF/HP.
- **Cavalry** — most balanced ATK/DEF/HP PLUS **march speed boost** (unique).

### Recommendation (VERIFIED — most favored by community)

**Cavalry** — Best for Raiders:
- Faster to attack + return + raid resources.
- During KvK: raid a resource hoarder → enemy ports in with mixed/infantry → your Cavalry raids and returns to defend **before** enemy reaches your castle.
- Recon and raid **further** from random teleports.
- Well-balanced stats + march speed = flexibility.

**Ranged** — High Attack, Rally Support:
- High-attack meta pick.
- Also useful supporting a Cav-majority alliance: pairing Ranged with T-tier-differentiated Cav rallies reduces troop loss (siblings of Cav in rally efficiency).

**Infantry** — NOT recommended for most:
- Requires **heavy money spending** to strengthen effectively.
- For high spenders only → becomes a mighty adversary at scale.
- ⚠️ Do NOT specialize Infantry just because you got a **Sarbor** early. Heroes all come in time. **150 Diamonds resets a hero** — cheap redirect if you invested wrong.

### Training Rule for Specialized Play
- **ONLY use speedups on your chosen troop type.** Other troops stay in slow-train indefinitely.
- **Never stop training the other types** — you'll still use fewer slow-trained troops for rallies, garrison, and specific event tricks.

## Specialization — How to Boost the Chosen Type

### Gear + Equipment
**Only promote/enhance gear for your chosen troop type far beyond the others.**

For **Cavalry** (example):
- **Sword + Shield** → Cavalry ATK
- **Helmet + Ring** → Cavalry DEF/HP

**Rule**: Keep other-troop-type gear 1–2 stars and 2–3 enhances **behind** your specialized set. They still get some love because you'll deploy those slow-train troops in mixed contexts.

Also applies to **Gems · Saurgems · Curios · Warsigils** — always favor the specialized troop line.

### Hero Types (Battle Heroes)
Two categories:

**Full-boost MONO heroes (4★ + 5★)**: Give **3 battle stats** — HP + DEF + ATK to ONE troop type.

**Partial-boost BI heroes (3★ + 4★)**: Give **DEF + ATK** to one troop type PLUS **1 economy boost**.

**For Cavalry**:
- **Full-boost (mono)**: 5★ Lux · 5★ Guan Yu · 4★ Malice · 3★ Talon
- **Partial-boost (bi)**: 4★ Apollo · 3★ Dracula · 2★ Naax

### Ideal Cavalry Team (Progression)

**Early-mid game team**: **Lux · Guan Yu · Malice · Apollo · Talon**

**Why keep 3★ Talon at +5?** — **Tactical Skill at hero +5 = 500% ATK boost** to your troop ATK%. This is a **multiplier** on top of your existing troop ATK.

**All-Troop heroes** (Skender · Yang · Medusa etc.) have LOWER base ATK/DEF/HP than mono heroes but share the same Tactical Skill scaling.
- **Result**: **Yang +4 (300% tactical) will NOT outdamage Talon +5 (500% tactical)** — until Yang also reaches +5, UNLESS your troop ATK% is already above roughly **200%+**.
- Once past that threshold OR once Yang/Skender/etc reach +5, they become the correct pick because they buff ALL troops in your march.

**Late-game team**: **Lux · Guan Yu · Malice · Skender · Yang**
- Malice eventually replaced by newer 5★ Cav hero **Cleopatra** OR a much higher-enhance 5★ all-troop hero worthy enough to lose the Tactical Cav bonus.

### Hero Leveling Efficiency Rule
Unless using 3★/4★ heroes in **Arena or Puzzles**, only level them up to the level required to unlock the **Troop ATK skill**:
- 3★ → **Lv.71**
- 4★ + 5★ → **Lv.131**

**Everything past that on non-5★ = wasted Soulstones.** Reset them, refund Soulstones, redirect to your 5★s.

**5★ heroes** you use in **Puzzles + Arena Championships** → keep leveling up to **Lv.350** for puzzle purposes.

### Curio Equipping Rule
Ideal target: **All your 5★ heroes to Lv.211+** to equip **Curios** for the highest-tier boosts.

**Doctrine summary**: Pick one troop (Cav for most, Ranged if high attack focus, Infantry only for heavy spenders). Only speedup-train that type. Promote gear/gems/saurgems/curios/warsigils primarily for the chosen type. Use mono 4★/5★ + partial-boost 3★+5 heroes early (Talon's Tactical +5 = 500% ATK multiplier), transition to Skender/Yang/all-troop meta at higher troop ATK% or when they reach +5. Reset non-5★s past skill-unlock threshold. Get 5★s to Lv.211+ for curios.

## Farms — Alt-Account Resource Generation Doctrine (VERIFIED)
Farms are dedicated alt accounts built to feed resources to your main castle.

### Farm Creation Rules
- **1 account per kingdom, per device** — you cannot make 2 accounts in the same kingdom.
- **Workaround**: Create accounts in **OTHER** kingdoms → migrate them to your main's kingdom.
- **Path**: More (bottom-left "+") → Settings → Manage Character → New Character → pick a kingdom to spawn the farm in.

### Farm Migration
**Below Castle Lv.6**:
- Use the free **Starter Teleport** the game grants: **works until Castle Lv.5** AND within **7 days of account creation** AND only when moving to a kingdom where you do NOT already have a castle.

**Above Castle Lv.5**:
- **Global Teleport** via **Alliance Shop** — costs **500,000 Alliance Coins**.
- **$5 pack** in Mall (unlocks after **C15**) grants **1 Global Teleport**.

### Farm Building Layout (35-building cap — VERIFIED)
- **1 Food Mine**
- **1 Wood Mine**
- **8 Iron Mines**
- **8 Gold Mines**
- **7 Hospitals**
- **10 Training Camps**

**Why the imbalance?** Food + Wood are cheap and generate fast from monsters/hell forts already. Iron + Gold are the bottlenecks — max them. Hospitals + Training Camps support troop rebuilds after wipe.

### Why Hospitals on a Market-Only Farm?
Even if you send resources via Market (not by attacking your farm), hospitals still matter because:
- Farms **gather** on tiles → gather troops can be attacked while out.
- Wounded gathering troops need Hospital slots or they're **DEAD**.
- Losing 100% of your farm's gather troops = 24+ hours of retraining downtime.

### Training Camps — Ongoing Need
- **Yes, all the time.** Farms constantly lose troops (attacks by main, hell fort wipes, wounded troops that overflow hospital cap).
- Also used for the Alt-Coordination Monster Hunt trick (1-cav-per-farm hit for reward distribution — see Monster Hunt doctrine).

### Optimal Farm Castle Level: **C22** (VERIFIED)
- **C22** = the sweet spot for farms.
- Reason 1: **Unlocks T8 catapults** → gather MORE per march load.
- Reason 2: T8 cats give **~2 days of gathering time with a full march** — massive uptime per march.
- Reason 3: Post-C22, training/transport cost climbs faster than benefit. Above C22 = diminishing returns for farm purpose.

### F2P vs Paid Farms
- **F2P farm is 100% viable** — everything (Global Teleports via alliance coins, VIP via daily login + alliance shop points, gear/tech via time) is earnable without paying.
- Paid farms grow faster but the ceiling is the same. F2P just takes longer.

### Market — Send Resources Home
- Upgrade the **Market** on the farm to **same level as its Castle** — max transport size + reduced tax.
- Sending resources to your main via Market costs a tax on delta between distances/levels.

### Gathering Speed Optimization
**Train Cavalry** for faster marches OR **Catapults** for larger load capacity (higher payload = fewer march cycles needed to deplete a tile).

**Institute (Tech)**: Focus **Economy** category — grants gathering speed + higher output from castle's own mines.

**Sauroi (Economy Skill)**: Level Food/Wood/Iron/Gold **gathering speed** + **Troop Load** — troop load reduces the number of troops needed to clear a resource tile.

### Farm Attack Tax (VERIFIED CLARIFICATION)
Many players attack their own farms to skip the Market tax on resource transfer.
- ⚠️ **The attack-your-farm method is NOT tax-free**.
- **When you attack a castle, you lose 30% of the resources you plunder** to the attack tax.
- Compare the **attacker's** post-battle report vs. the **defender's** to verify — the delta is the 30% tax.
- Many players eat the tax anyway because attacking is **faster** than sending many individual Market shipments.
- **Doctrine**: Market for large, planned shipments (lower tax, higher volume). Attack for speed when you need resources fast and can absorb the 30%.

## Territory Overview + Held vs Bag Resources
Territory Overview reports each resource's: Held · Daily Cap · Hourly Output · In Bag.

**Held Resources** = "ready cash" — pays first, **plunderable** above Storage limit.
- Ways to reduce held: spend · send to Alliance Storage · Mystery Shop exchange (moves to Bag).
- Ways held increases: gather (Perimeter/World Map) · withdraw from Alliance Storage · Fast Producer talent · Goddess Statue Wish · Alliance gift (huge tax).

**Total in Bag** = safe/unraidable. Must be withdrawn to spend.
- Bag increases via: event rewards · Mystery Shop · Rare Earth Shop · Alliance Gifts.

**Daily Cap** = only 10 hours' worth of hourly output (out of 24).
**Fast Producer talent:** 5× hourly output, does NOT count against Daily Cap. Activatable 2× daily = effectively doubles the cap.

### Gate of Abyss — FULL DOCTRINE
**Objective**: Defeat the **Abyss Legion** at the Gate of Abyss within a time limit. Alliance members rally against the gate; rewards distributed by **damage dealt** after gate closes.

Higher-level abyss = more HP → needs stronger alliance to burn it down, but pays out better.

**Core loop**: Start a rally. Join every rally you see. Repeat until gate closes.

**Best-practice tactics (verified)**:

**1. Location (post-update)** — March time is a fixed **30 seconds** each way regardless of position. **Abyss location no longer matters.** Do not waste diamonds relocating for this.

**2. Everyone starts a rally.** Max **5 rallies per player** simultaneously. 5-minute rally window + march time included. More rallies from more players = more damage burst. Approximate stagger:
- 30–25 min → 1st rally
- 24–19 min → 2nd rally
- 18–13 min → 3rd rally
- 12–7 min → 4th rally
- 6–1 min → 5th rally

**3. Fill rallies with whatever you have.** Even catapults work if you don't have 5 full marches of infantry/cavalry/ranged. **Avoid catapults unless necessary.** Sending catapults does **NOT** affect the 30-second march time (universal).

**4. Send your best 5 marches with heroes assigned.**

**5. Damage-Point Optimization (advanced)** — Big players joining your rally will **steal your damage points**. Two ways to defend against this:

**Option A — Threshold cap**: There are damage-reward phases. Find your personal max phase (e.g. "I know my ceiling is 500K–700K damage and I can't reach the next phase"). Once you hit your ceiling, **stop sending troops** to Gate of Abyss — any further damage is wasted AND stolen from others. But still start a rally with **1 cavalry** so others can join it.
- ⚠️ Hectic: requires self-restraint, coordination, and finding your damage phase (changes as you grow).

**Option B — Two-tier rally split**: Small players start rallies + only small players join them (damage even). Strong players start their own rallies + only strong players join those (damage even at the top).
- ⚠️ Hard to run: strong players earn less than usual, needs coordination, needs enough players online for both tiers.

*(Skip Option A/B entirely if damage rewards aren't your priority.)*

**6. Hall of War level = Castle level.** Keeps your rally troop count maxed. Underleveled HoW = fewer troops per rally = fewer rallies fill = alliance loses damage.

**7. Join the rally with the LEAST time remaining first** — as long as there's still room for your troops. Prioritize filling almost-departing rallies over starting fresh ones.

**8. Always start a rally if none are available.** Empty rally slot = your alliance mates have nowhere to send troops.

**9. Do NOT join a rally if your march time exceeds the rally's remaining time.** You'll slow everyone down and possibly cost the alliance a full rally launch.

**10. Do NOT join an almost-full rally with too few troops.** Rule of thumb: if rally is nearly full AND you can fit **75%+ of your troop count**, joining is safe. Below 75% = better to start your own.

### Monster Hunt — FULL DOCTRINE
**Objective**: Kill as many monsters as possible in the shortest time. Alliance-coordinated event.

**Event Structure**:
- Event starts → **yellow circles** appear on map = monster spawn points.
- After a countdown, **monsters spawn inside the circles**.
- **One circle stays empty at first** → once all other monsters are killed, the **BOSS** spawns in that empty circle.
- Kill the boss → alliance receives **1 gift chest** + circles refill with fresh monsters.
- **Each round, monsters get slightly stronger.**
- Event runs **30 minutes**: smaller monsters alternate with boss waves throughout.

**Fixed march time**: Distance from monster **does not matter** — marches are hard-locked at **30 seconds each way** (30s out + 30s back). Location tricks are irrelevant.

**Core coordination doctrine**:
- In **later rounds** (stronger bosses), multiple players should send **multiple marches** at the boss.
- ⚠️ **Do NOT overcommit** — overkill wastes troops that need to be back in the castle immediately to attack the next monster wave.

**Pre-event tip — Battle Frenzy activation**:
- Before Monster Hunt starts, **scout/attack a foreign castle** to activate **Battle Frenzy** — grants a short-duration **percentage bonus to your troops**. Enter the event with the buff active.

**Alliance positioning trick**:
- If your alliance is in the Lost Land and you want to farm monsters in the **black area** (or vice versa), the **event starter** can briefly relocate their castle to the target zone and trigger the event.
- Result: all monsters + boss spawn in that zone. Cleaner for players who prefer that area.

**Advanced tactics (verified community intel)**:

**1. T11+ Boss Solo Doctrine**
- If your alliance has a castle with **T11+ troops**, assign them to **focus the boss alone**.
- Everyone else keeps **one march free for regular monsters** — parallelizes the point flow.
- **A strong T11 can solo the boss with 1 march up to Wave 10**, and **with 3 marches up to Wave 12**.

**2. Trigger-Castle Isolation (spot clustering)**
- Have an **R4** (or your event triggerer) **port to the black land** (or anywhere with few castles nearby).
- Trigger the event from that isolated position.
- Result: monster spots pop **around the triggering castle** — the fewer castles nearby, the **tighter the cluster**. Faster reaction times for everyone.

**3. Recall Talent / Direct Relocator abuse**
- **Recall skill** or **Direct Relocator** brings all marches back instantly.
- Combo: port near the trigger castle → send marches → recall instantly → send again.
- Effectively doubles or triples your action tempo per wave.

**4. Auto vs. Tactical Send**
- Early waves: safe to send using **Auto** to move fast.
- Learn where your army breaks — that's where you switch to **tactical marches** (specific heroes, army composition).

**5. Late-wave 35-second delay**
- Have **1–2 strong castles** wait **35 seconds** before sending in **late waves**.
- These reserves focus on monsters **not killed** by the first wave of marches → prevents leftover monsters from stalling wave progression.

**6. Main + Farm coordination (multi-account)**
- If you play a **main + a farm**, designate a specific monster spot where **all farms send 1 cav**, and where the **main sends a full march 5 seconds after Wave 1 starts**.
- Result: all castles score at least 1 hit → **everyone gets full rewards**.
- Cost: 5 seconds of delay. Reward: 100% loot distribution.

**Doctrine summary**: Coordination > raw troop count. Assign boss duty, isolate spawn cluster, abuse Recall for tempo, delay reserves for cleanup, and if running alt farms, always structure the 1-cav-per-farm hit for reward guarantees.

## Alliance Conquest — Battle Phase (verified)
Battle phase lasts **24 hours**. Each participating player gets **2 attack windows** in that period.
- **Each successful attack = 100 Alliance Points.**
- Only the **30 players selected** by alliance leadership can participate in the battle phase.
- Non-participating members see a **Spectate** button to watch battles in real time — they cannot attack or reinforce during the battle phase.

**Line-up Shield System:**
- **Green shield** on an enemy line-up = you can attack it.
- **Red shield** on an enemy line-up = already defeated, locked out, cannot be attacked again.
- Player picks WHICH enemy line-up to fight.
- **Win** the fight → their shield turns **red** (line-up removed from attackable pool) + your alliance banks the 100 points.
- **Lose** the fight → you burn 1 attack attempt · alliance scores 0 · shield stays **green** · that line-up remains attackable by others.

**Line-up Rule (defender side):**
- Each defender fields **2 line-ups**. Each can only be used **once**.
- If your first line-up already fought, you can't reuse it — the second line-up must defend.
- When **both** your line-ups are defeated, you're **locked out** of further attacks against you (no more incoming).

**Battle Record:**
- Post-phase log shows who fought whom, who won, who lost — accountability tool for both alliances.
- Use it to identify weak links inside your own alliance AND top threats inside the enemy alliance for next cycle.

- Strategy: burn both attacks on high-value targets to maximize the 200-point per-player ceiling. Coordinate rally targets via alliance chat before battle-phase open to avoid double-hitting the same target. Study battle records after each cycle to refine matchmaking — don't send weak line-ups against known enemy tanks.
