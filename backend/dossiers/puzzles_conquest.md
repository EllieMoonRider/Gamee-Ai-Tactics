## Game Overview
Mythology-themed match-3 strategy. City-builder + kingdom warfare + match-3 combat hybrid. Alliances for protection. Outside the city: resources, monsters, hell fortresses (daily quests). **Altar of Dragonia** = central map objective — alliances fight for control; holder gains kingdom-leader bonuses. **Hero Hall** to recruit heroes. Heroes: level up + combine to strengthen. Match-3 mini-game: each hero has a color = matching gem type; 3+ in a row/column attacks enemies.

Tagline: "Crush, Construct, Conquer".

## Alliance Help / Speedup
- Teammates speed up your building/training/researching/healing at NO cost to you.
- **~1 minute reduction per help** (varies with help efficiency).
- Reward for helping others: **800 Alliance Coins per help** · daily cap 40,000.
- Daily Quest: help 10× = 5 pts.
- **Speedup Help Cap** = max teammates that can help ONE task, governed ONLY by Embassy level:
  - No Embassy: 2 helpers
  - Embassy L1: 4 helpers · each upgrade increases both count + efficiency
- **CRITICAL:** Cap is locked at Alliance Hall level when the task STARTED, NOT current level.

## Territory Overview + Held vs Bag Resources
Territory Overview reports each resource's: Held · Daily Cap · Hourly Output · In Bag.

**Held Resources** = "ready cash" — pays first, **plunderable** above Storage limit.
- Ways to reduce held: spend · send to Alliance Storage · Mystery Shop exchange (moves to Bag).
- Ways held increases: gather (Perimeter/World Map) · withdraw from Alliance Storage · Fast Producer talent · Goddess Statue Wish · Alliance gift (huge tax).

**Total in Bag** = safe/unraidable. Must be withdrawn to spend.
- Bag increases via: event rewards · Mystery Shop · Rare Earth Shop · Alliance Gifts.

**Daily Cap** = only 10 hours' worth of hourly output (out of 24).
**Fast Producer talent:** 5× hourly output, does NOT count against Daily Cap. Activatable 2× daily = effectively doubles the cap.

### Demon Incursion (PvE) — FULL DOCTRINE
**Cadence**: Even weeks (Week 2 / 4 / 6…). Runs Mon+Wed OR Tue+Thu depending on your time zone.

**Structure**: 20 waves total launched from infernal fortresses.
- **Waves 1–9, 11–19**: attack individual castles.
- **Waves 10 and 20**: attack the **Alliance Fort** (NOT individual castles).
- Higher difficulty levels increase both the number and type of attacking troops. **Elite troops** appear in higher waves (Wave 14 and Wave 17 are elite point tiers) and hit especially hard on attack — respect them.

**Personal-max play (all members)**:
- Deploy all **5 marches** as reinforcements to weaker castles → each reinforcement earns points.
- On **Wave 10** and **Wave 20**, recall **1 march** and send it to reinforce the **Alliance Fort** for extra points.

**Alliance-strategy fork — set doctrine BEFORE event start**:

**Option A: Maximum Alliance ranking (TOP 10 push)**
- **Strongest player enters the Alliance Fort first** (legion size matters).
- Other top players fill in with **full marches**.
- Remaining space can be filled with token troops (e.g. 1K).
- ⚠️ **Total Fort score is divided among ALL castles inside the Fort** — more castles = fewer points per player.
- ⚠️ Small castles inside the Fort that will drop below 50% twice waste allocation slots — once you're below 50% twice, you've lost your fight and won't receive further points.
- **DOCTRINE**: Only put castles in the Fort that can actually cash in the Fort points.

**Option B: Even distribution (points for everyone)**
- Strongest player still enters the Fort first (legion size).
- All other players enter with a **predetermined troop count** so points distribute proportionally to strength across the whole alliance.

**Personal Rank-1 tactic (Froth1k, verified)**:
- Difficulty: **Level 7 or Level 11**. Level 8 = wasted effort.
- Set **farms ~250–260 km** from castle. Pre-reinforce farms with each other (locks other players out from stealing them).
- Wait for **Wave 17 (elite)** to reach the farms.
- Use **Recall Talent** to pull troops back fast · redeploy to castles/farms → defend again (faster than most demon marches).
- **Wave 14 = elite · Wave 17 = elite** — the highest point tiers.
- After Wave 17: most points banked, pull back.
- **DO NOT** burn diamonds on Direct Relocators or Speedups. Rank 1 target = ~5,000 Diamonds spent.

**Extra-points tricks (advanced)**:
1. **Ghost-reinforce inactive castles post-event**: If your alliance has very-far inactive castles, they can still be reinforced *after* the battle formally ends — troops en route may still count. After Wave 20 completes, port your castle near a distant inactive castle and reinforce it while marches are still in transit.
2. **Empty-castle 100% score trick**: If a smaller reinforced castle uses its own troops to reinforce yet another (inactive) castle, so the small castle you reinforced is now "empty", the reinforcer gets **100% of the points** for defending it. This is the max possible score per defense event.

**PRIORITY ORDER (personal)**: Rank 7/11 farm-lock tactic > Fort reinforcement on Wave 10/20 > Ghost-reinforce inactives post-event > Standard 5-march reinforcement rotation.

### Dragondom Conquest (Quarterly Mega-Event) — FULL DOCTRINE
**Cadence**: Approximately **once per quarter**, runs for **2 months**.
**Entry requirement**: Castle **Lv.29+**.

**3 Stages (sequential)**:

| Stage | Prep | Battle | Grouping |
|-------|------|--------|----------|
| **1. Overlord War** | 30 min | 2 hr 30 min | Kingdoms split into **6 groups** |
| **2. Grand Warden War** | 30 min | 3 hr 30 min | Kingdoms split into **3 groups** |
| **3. Supreme King War** | 30 min | 4 hr 30 min | **NO groups** — all kingdoms compete |

**Winner per group** = player with the **longest cumulative occupation time** as captain of the fortresses during the battle.

**Each stage has 3 phases**:

1. **Voting Period**: The **top 10 most powerful lords** in each kingdom (within the group) vote from proposed battle times. Highest-voted time wins.
2. **Battle Period**: When battle opens, a button appears in the lower-right corner — tap to enter the designated map.
   - **Ticket cost** (Diamond Shop / Bag):
     - Overlord War ticket = **10,000 Diamonds**
     - Grand Warden War ticket = **20,000 Diamonds**
     - **Advantageous alternative**: Dragondom Shop for **100,000 Exploit** — much better than diamond spend.
     - **Pro tip**: You can buy a **Supreme King War ticket** from the Dragondom Shop and save **20,000 Diamonds** — a strong investment.
   - Once ticketed, move your castle to the map. **Position close to the Cursed Land** so you can intercept marches heading for altars/fortress.
   - ⚠️ **DANGER**: If ANY part of your castle sits ON the Cursed Land, **anyone can attack it** — even during the 30-min preparation window.
   - ⚠️ **Wounded units do NOT go to the Dragondom Infirmary** while in preparation phase.
3. **End of Battle**: Occupation time tallied → winner announced.

**Battle Mechanics**:
- Map contains **4 altars + 1 fortress**.
- During the 30-min prep, altars + fortress are protected by **Shield of Grace** — unattackable (except castles on Cursed Land).
- After prep ends, Shields drop → fortress battle begins.
- **Fortress winner** = longest total occupation (need not be continuous). Occupying **>50% of round duration** = guaranteed win.
- When altars are captured by an **enemy alliance**, they deal **3% damage per minute** to the number of soldiers in the fortress.
- After battle, wounded units go to **Dragondom Infirmary** (except during prep phase — those are lost).

**Quests during battle** → yield **Exploit** currency for the Dragondom Shop. **Cap: 2 million Exploit per war**. Exploit persists across up to **3 wars**, but is wiped when the full event ends.

**Rewards (3 types)**:
1. **Titles** distributed by the Ruler to participants + chests with rewards.
2. **Feast** (see below).
3. **Quest rewards** — more quests completed = more rewards.

**Feasts (Ruler-only power)**:
- Overlord = **5 feasts**, Grand Warden = **10 feasts**, Supreme King = **15 feasts**.
- Only holdable **during** the Dragondom Conquest event.
- **3 feast variants** — different duration + host reward.
- **Ruler tips**:
  1. **Do NOT rush** — best attendance is **Saturday and Sunday**.
  2. Announce feast time in advance. Use **delayed launch** to maximize attendance.
  3. More participants + more purchases at feast = more rewards for **everyone**.

**Dragondom Shop — Buying Doctrine**:
⚠️ **Shop does NOT refresh during the event.** Plan purchases.

**Buy priority (verified)**:
1. **RARE items** that are hard/impossible to get elsewhere. **Chest of rare Scrolls** = high value. **Spar** = ONLY obtainable here.
2. Purchase **Spinels / Promoters** during Lord Event windows for extra points.
3. **Save up** enough Exploit to buy the unique rewards + **Enhancement Materials chests + Alchemist Handbooks + Cryptic Engineering + Curio** — highly efficient.
4. **Supreme King War ticket** — buy from shop, saves 20,000 Diamonds.
5. **Speed-ups** — LOW priority. Only buy if nothing else useful remains.

**Doctrine summary**: This is a **team event**. Invite alliance members to participate — combined participation multiplies feast rewards and coordinated fortress holds are how titles get won.

### Gate of Abyss — FULL DOCTRINE
**Objective**: Defeat the **Abyss Legion** at the Gate of Abyss within a time limit. Alliance members rally against the gate; rewards distributed by **damage dealt** after gate closes.

**Rewards** (scale with damage AND with abyss level):
- **Oath Runes II or III** (rune tier depends on Abyss Lord level)
- Alliance Coins
- Saurgem Essence
- Food · Wood · Iron · Gold
- **Saurgem Spinels** (unlocks at **10K+ damage**)
- **8★ Saurgem Promoters** (unlocks at **500K+ damage**)

Higher-level abyss = more HP → needs stronger alliance to burn it down, but pays out better.

**Core loop**: Start a rally. Join every rally you see. Repeat until gate closes.

**Best-practice tactics (verified)**:

**1. Location (post-update)** — March time is a fixed **30 seconds** each way regardless of position. **Abyss location no longer matters.** Do not waste diamonds relocating for this.

**2. Everyone starts a rally.** Max **5 rallies per player** simultaneously. 5-minute rally window + march time included. More rallies from more players = more damage burst. Approximate stagger:
- 30–25 min → 1st rally
- 24–19 min → 2nd rally
- 18–13 min → 3rd rally
- 12–7 min → 4th rally
- 6–1 min → 5th rally

**3. Fill rallies with whatever you have.** Even catapults work if you don't have 5 full marches of infantry/cavalry/ranged. **Avoid catapults unless necessary.** Sending catapults does **NOT** affect the 30-second march time (universal).

**4. Send your best 5 marches with heroes assigned.**

**5. Damage-Point Optimization (advanced)** — Big players joining your rally will **steal your damage points**. Two ways to defend against this:

**Option A — Threshold cap**: There are damage-reward phases. Find your personal max phase (e.g. "I know my ceiling is 500K–700K damage and I can't reach the next phase"). Once you hit your ceiling, **stop sending troops** to Gate of Abyss — any further damage is wasted AND stolen from others. But still start a rally with **1 cavalry** so others can join it.
- ⚠️ Hectic: requires self-restraint, coordination, and finding your damage phase (changes as you grow).

**Option B — Two-tier rally split**: Small players start rallies + only small players join them (damage even). Strong players start their own rallies + only strong players join those (damage even at the top).
- ⚠️ Hard to run: strong players earn less than usual, needs coordination, needs enough players online for both tiers.

*(Skip Option A/B entirely if damage rewards aren't your priority.)*

**6. Hall of War level = Castle level.** Keeps your rally troop count maxed. Underleveled HoW = fewer troops per rally = fewer rallies fill = alliance loses damage.

**7. Join the rally with the LEAST time remaining first** — as long as there's still room for your troops. Prioritize filling almost-departing rallies over starting fresh ones.

**8. Always start a rally if none are available.** Empty rally slot = your alliance mates have nowhere to send troops.

**9. Do NOT join a rally if your march time exceeds the rally's remaining time.** You'll slow everyone down and possibly cost the alliance a full rally launch.

**10. Do NOT join an almost-full rally with too few troops.** Rule of thumb: if rally is nearly full AND you can fit **75%+ of your troop count**, joining is safe. Below 75% = better to start your own.

## Alliance Showdown Event — Inefficient-Task Table (Jungleboogie, verified)
Some Alliance Showdown tasks pay LESS points per unit of effort than alternate tasks. Avoid these unless mandatory or as a specific alt-account play.

**INEFFICIENT tasks (do NOT prioritize):**
- **Resource Gather**: 113 pts — same output as 138 pts task with less time investment.
- **Resource Transport**: ALL point values — typically best for farms only, otherwise inefficient.
- **Get Materials (Gear/Gem/Saurgem/Warsigil)**: 113 pts — requires 2× the amount vs. the 120 pts task.
- **Mystery Shop**: 100 pts — requires 4 more purchases than the 122 pts version.
- **Training Quantity**: ALL — inefficient; Troop Might quests are easier and pay more even with T1.
- **Troop/Build/Tech Might**: 113 pts — requires 2× the amount vs. 120 pts.
- **Speedup**: ALL — Might upgrades are more efficient unless someone is ready to complete a big upgrade.
- **Helps**: 109 pts — requires 20 more helps than the 127 pts version.
- **Hero Frags**: 113 pts — requires 2× the amount vs. 120 pts.
- **Consume Stamina**: 104 pts — requires 250 more than the 118 pts version.
- **Heal Units**: ALL — inefficient.

**DOCTRINE**: When Mystery Tasks roll, always compare point values before committing — if two similar tasks appear with wildly different point values but similar effort, take the higher-point one.

## Alliance Conquest — Battle Phase (verified)
Battle phase lasts **24 hours**. Each participating player gets **2 attack windows** in that period.
- **Each successful attack = 100 Alliance Points.**
- Only the **30 players selected** by alliance leadership can participate in the battle phase.
- Non-participating members see a **Spectate** button to watch battles in real time — they cannot attack or reinforce during the battle phase.

**Line-up Shield System:**
- **Green shield** on an enemy line-up = you can attack it.
- **Red shield** on an enemy line-up = already defeated, locked out, cannot be attacked again.
- Player picks WHICH enemy line-up to fight.
- **Win** the fight → their shield turns **red** (line-up removed from attackable pool) + your alliance banks the 100 points.
- **Lose** the fight → you burn 1 attack attempt · alliance scores 0 · shield stays **green** · that line-up remains attackable by others.

**Line-up Rule (defender side):**
- Each defender fields **2 line-ups**. Each can only be used **once**.
- If your first line-up already fought, you can't reuse it — the second line-up must defend.
- When **both** your line-ups are defeated, you're **locked out** of further attacks against you (no more incoming).

**Battle Record:**
- Post-phase log shows who fought whom, who won, who lost — accountability tool for both alliances.
- Use it to identify weak links inside your own alliance AND top threats inside the enemy alliance for next cycle.

- Strategy: burn both attacks on high-value targets to maximize the 200-point per-player ceiling. Coordinate rally targets via alliance chat before battle-phase open to avoid double-hitting the same target. Study battle records after each cycle to refine matchmaking — don't send weak line-ups against known enemy tanks.
